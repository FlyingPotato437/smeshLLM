# SmeshLLM - Wildfire Smoke Prediction Platform

SmeshLLM is Stanford University's comprehensive wildfire smoke prediction platform that combines physics-informed AI with real-time sensor data and advanced visualization tools.

## Core Features

- **Physics-Informed AI**: HYSPLIT integration with transformer models for accurate smoke plume prediction
- **Real-Time Sensor Network**: Raspberry Pi sensors providing live air quality and environmental data
- **3D Visualization**: Interactive smoke plume visualization using Deck.gl and satellite data
- **AI Assistant**: Conversational interface for querying wildfire and air quality data
- **Research Platform**: Tools for atmospheric modeling and scientific analysis

## Key Components

- **Dashboard**: Real-time monitoring of air quality and fire conditions
- **Chat Interface**: AI-powered assistant for data queries and analysis
- **Visualization**: 3D smoke plume modeling and interactive maps
- **Sensor Management**: Integration with IoT devices and data ingestion
- **Research Tools**: Physics-informed neural networks and atmospheric modeling

## Target Users

- Researchers studying wildfire behavior and air quality
- Emergency response teams monitoring fire conditions
- Environmental scientists analyzing atmospheric data
- Public users seeking air quality information
